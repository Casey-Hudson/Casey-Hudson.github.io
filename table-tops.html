Table Tops

Unsatisfied with the state of mainstream tabletop RPGs, I set out with my team to design my own system.

Our aim in designing this system is to maximise player customisation by broadening the character's starting point, and creative freedom throughout the game as it progresses. Taking advantage of the general audiences disatisfaction with the lack of creative expression in mainstream games, and the boom in new game sytsems, we intend to deliver a completely player focused experience

Currently in our conceptual phase, we are writing game rules and world lore concurrently; defining the scope of our world for our players, and the means to interact with it, in it's entirety.
