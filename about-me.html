About me

I love anything with a narrative. Books, movies, tv-series, video games, comics - whatever. If it has a narrative I'm invested in it and tearing it apart. I'm a firm believer that no good story comes without the bad, and no bad without some good, and finding out where both lie is my most valuable skill.

Achieving consistency is something I strive for in my own writing, and this desire has become another useful skill of mine. I have always been critical, even as a child, and this ability to scrutinise will be invaluable for editing work.

As it stands I am an amateur writer, but from here is where the most growth can be achieved.
